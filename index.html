⚡ Turn Off Battery Optimization
To receive Water & Diet Reminders on your lock screen even when the app is closed, Android requires turning off battery optimization for your browser:
📱 Chrome / PWA Settings:
1. Open Android Settings → Apps → Chrome (or Suryasarthi 108).
2. Tap Battery → Select "Unrestricted".
3. Tap Notifications → Enable "Allow sound and vibration" & "Lock screen: Show content".
📲 Samsung (One UI):
1. Settings → Apps → Chrome / App → Battery → Choose "Unrestricted".
2. Settings → Battery → Background usage limits → Add Chrome to "Never sleeping apps".
📱 Xiaomi / Redmi (MIUI / HyperOS):
1. Settings → Apps → Manage Apps → Chrome / App.
2. Turn ON "Autostart" → Tap Battery Saver → Choose "No Restrictions".
📲 OnePlus / Oppo / RealMe:
1. Settings → Battery → App Battery Management → Select Chrome / App.
2. Enable "Allow background activity" & "Allow auto-launch".
